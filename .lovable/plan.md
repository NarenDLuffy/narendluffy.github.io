# Get more visitors to 3GPP Companion

## What the numbers show (Nov 16–23, 2026)
- 527 visits. Most were direct (460), 63 came from links, 4 from search or AI tools, and none from social.
- Traffic peaks during meeting week. People mostly use Schedule, Home and My agenda.
- Your visitors are 3GPP delegates, a small, well-defined group. Word of mouth inside companies works best for them, not ads or SEO.

## Ranked actions
1. **Share with each working group during its meeting week.** Post a short message in delegate chats (Teams/WeChat/email) a few days before RAN1/RAN2/RAN3/RAN4/SA1/SA2/RAN Plenary meetings start. Each message gets a tagged link (utm_source=teams, utm_medium=social) so we can see which posts bring people in.
2. **Ask early users to pass it on.** Send a two-line message to 5–10 colleagues who already use it, asking each to share it with one delegate from another group. Include a feedback question: "What stopped you from using it in the room?"
3. **Ask chairs/secretaries for permission to mention it.** A short note asking whether a link can go in the meeting-arrangements reflector mail or the chair notes. Only after they agree.
4. **LinkedIn post after a meeting week**, with a screenshot of the Now page and real usage numbers (e.g. "used by ~100 delegates per day at RAN1#127").
5. **Small app fix:** add a "Share with a colleague" button on Schedule (copies the tagged link), so sharing happens at the moment people find the app useful.
6. Later: decide on the 3gpplive.net vs ran1.app domain before promoting it widely, so links don't change after people save them.

Skipped: paid ads and SEO (the audience is too small and specialised; almost nobody searches for this).
